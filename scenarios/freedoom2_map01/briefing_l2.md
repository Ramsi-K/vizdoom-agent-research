## Objective
Reach the level exit and activate its switch before the episode timeout.

## Core mechanics
Press use while close (about 64 map units) and facing a door, switch or lift to interact. Doors may stay open temporarily; switches can move geometry. Colored locks need matching keys. Walking over items collects them. Hazardous slime can hurt and medkits heal. Monsters are disabled as a navigation-only experimental control.

## Failure modes
Player death, episode timeout, or a harness budget ending before exit activation.

## Exclusions
No route, turn sequence, compass headings, or coordinates are supplied. The exit's position has not been annotated.
