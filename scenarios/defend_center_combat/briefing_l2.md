## Objective
Remain alive until the full 2,100-tic scenario timeout in the bundled defend-the-center environment. Monsters are enabled at difficulty 3. A shorter harness limit does not establish survival of the full horizon.

## Mechanics
The HUD shows the current weapon, ammunition, and health. ATTACK fires; weapon controls request an owned weapon. Pickups apply on contact. Some exposed controls may have no effect in this map.

## Failure conditions
Player death, or a harness limit ending the attempt before the full survival horizon.

## Exclusions
No enemy locations, combat strategy, target priority, recommended movement pattern, or action sequence is provided.
