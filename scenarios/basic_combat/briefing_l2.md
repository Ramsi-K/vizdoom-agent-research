## Objective
Eliminate the target in this room before the 300-tic episode timeout. This is the bundled basic shooting scenario with monsters enabled and difficulty 3.

## Mechanics
The current weapon and ammunition are visible in the HUD. ATTACK fires the current weapon; weapon-slot/cycle controls request a switch to an owned weapon. Movement, view controls, and item collection follow the exposed controls. No weapon or ammunition is granted by the harness.

## Failure conditions
Player death, episode timeout, or a harness decision/cost/time limit before the objective is completed.

## Exclusions
No aiming direction, target location, combat tactic, action sequence, or route is supplied.
