## Objective
Reach and collect the green armor vest before the episode timeout.

## Core mechanics
Walking over the vest collects it automatically. Use interacts with nearby doors, switches and lifts when facing them. Colored locks need matching keys. Hazard floors can hurt; medkits heal. Monsters are disabled for this navigation-only configuration.

## Failure modes
Player death, timeout, or exhausted harness budgets.

## Exclusions
No route, turn sequence, compass headings, or coordinates are supplied.
