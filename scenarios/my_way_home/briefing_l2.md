## Objective
Collect the green armor vest (GreenArmor) before the 2,100-tic episode timeout. Walking over it ends the episode successfully.

## Core mechanics
Walking over pickups collects them without use. In Doom, use interacts with doors, switches and lifts when close (about 64 map units) and facing them. Colored locks may require matching keys. Hazardous floor slime can inflict damage; medkits heal while poison vials in hazard scenarios hurt. These are general interaction conventions, not a route through this level.

## Failure modes
Episode timeout or player death.
