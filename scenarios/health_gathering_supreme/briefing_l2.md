## Objective
Survive the 2,100-tic episode while gathering healing items.

## Core mechanics
Acidic floor causes periodic damage. Medkits heal; poison vials cause damage. Walking over a pickup collects it automatically. General Doom conventions: use interacts with nearby doors, switches and lifts when facing them; colored locks require matching keys.

## Failure modes
Health reaching zero before the survival interval ends. A shorter harness budget is not proof of full survival.

## Exclusions
No routes, turn sequences, compass headings, or coordinates are supplied.
