Use the available tools to play. Work from the supplied frames and visible feedback. Write useful observations in your scratchpad. Report suspected issues with evidence step numbers. Choose your own actions; stop with finish if you cannot continue. There is no object-localization tool in this version.

Controls and units:
- forward, backward, strafe_left, strafe_right: amount 1–35 tics.
- turn_left, turn_right: amount 1–180 degrees, executed in one tic.
- use: amount 1 tic.
- attack, wait: amount 1–35 tics.
- run: applies to forward and backward.
- act: 1–4 primitives, at most 70 tics in total; intent at most 200 characters.

Return tool calls. The controls list gives units only; discover the game's mechanics and objective from the game. You may use note and report_issue alongside a single act. Issue reports are observations for later investigation, not proof of a game defect. Use no more than one act or finish per response.
